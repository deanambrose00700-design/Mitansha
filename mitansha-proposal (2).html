A Letter

To My Princess, Mitansha

My Princess,

Or should I say... my favorite Confused Girl.

It's funny how life changes. Not with fireworks. Not with dramatic moments. Just quietly — until one day you realize someone has become the brightest part of your everyday life. That was you.

You didn't walk into my life trying to change it. Yet somehow, everything became a little more beautiful after you arrived.

Baby

There are moments I replay without even trying — your smile, your laughter, our conversations, the way you unknowingly make ordinary days feel extraordinary. I don't know the exact moment my heart chose you. Maybe it happened little by little, until thinking about you became my favorite habit.

Late Night Talking

You became the person I wanted to tell everything — the good news, the bad days, the random thoughts, the silly jokes, the dreams I haven't told anyone else. Even after our conversations end, I still find myself smiling.

Satellite

No matter how busy life becomes, my thoughts always find their way back to you — like they're searching for the place where they belong. And somehow, they always find you.

Sweet Creature

People think love has to be loud. I don't. I think it's found in the smallest things — remembering how you like your coffee, checking if you've eaten, calling you my favorite Confused Girl just to see your reaction, saying "good morning, baby," and finding peace simply because you're there.

Keep Driving

Life won't always be perfect. We'll have amazing days and difficult ones. We'll disagree, we'll grow, we'll make mistakes. But if there's one thing I hope never changes, it's that we keep choosing each other — again and again, through every season life gives us.

Adore You

There is so much I admire about you — your kindness, your heart, your smile, your strength, even the little things you probably don't notice about yourself. Loving someone like you makes me want to be worthy of them.

Love of My Life

I don't want a perfect love story. I want a real one — laughter, late-night conversations, inside jokes, warm hugs, comfort on hard days, and still teasing my favorite Confused Girl while calling you Baby, years from now.

The Beginning of Our Story

If someone asked me my favorite chapter in life, I'd say it hasn't been written yet — because I'm hoping we'll write it together.

So today, I'm not asking for perfection. I'm not asking for a fairy tale. I'm simply asking for a chance — to care for you, support your dreams, celebrate your victories, stand beside you when life gets hard, and keep calling you My Princess for a very, very long time.

— Yours, always
